Tutorials

The viewer draws an alignment, a tree and whatever annotations you hand it. Getting those three files is the part that takes the work, and it happens outside the browser, in tools built for it. Each page below is one continuous line of work — every step consumes what the step before produced — and ends on a URL that opens the result in JBrowseMSA.

The layers reference is the other half: how something you computed — per-column values, labeled highlights, pairs of positions, a structure correspondence — reaches the viewer as data rather than as a feature the viewer had to grow.
