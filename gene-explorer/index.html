Demo

JBrowse integration

Pick a species — human, mouse, zebrafish, fly, worm, plant, or yeast — and type a gene name. You get a connected JBrowse session: the gene on the genome (introns collapsed so the coding exons sit side by side) and its AlphaFold 3D structure, with human genes also carrying a 100-vertebrate protein alignment. Hover or select in any view and the others light up the matching residue — codon-precise, across genome, alignment, and structure. Everything is synthesized in the browser from public NCBI, UCSC GenArk, and AlphaFold data, with no per-gene files to host.
